# Manual voiceover test

Record yourself reading the script below (phone voice memo is fine), save it as **mp3, wav or m4a**,
then in clipsy: **Create → Voiceover → choose the file → Generate**.

Read at a normal pace, with a short pause between sentences. About **40 seconds**.

---

## Script to read (English)

> Every morning, the city wakes up before the sun.
> Coffee carts open on quiet street corners, and the first trains roll into the station.
> I used to think saving money meant giving up the things I love.
> Then I started tracking every rupee I spent.
> In one year, my savings grew forty percent.
> Now I sit by the river bank on Sundays, and I watch the clouds drift by.
> Trust is earned slowly — and so is a good habit.
> Start small. Start today.

## What to check in the result

| Line | What a good result shows | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| "city wakes up before the sun" | City skyline at dawn / sunrise | literal matching |
| "first trains roll into the station" | Train or station footage | literal matching |
| "tracking every rupee" | Money, budget, notebook or phone app | meaning, not keywords |
| "savings grew forty percent" | An animated **40%** stat card | statistics become cards |
| "river bank" | A river with grass/trees — **not** a bank building | double-meaning word |
| "watch the clouds drift by" | Sky with clouds — **not** servers | double-meaning word |
| "Trust is earned slowly" | A calm, metaphorical shot or a text card | abstract line |

Also check:
- Every cut lands between sentences, and the captions match your words.
- No scene repeats the same clip.
- The **9:16** version keeps the main subject in frame.
- The end of the video is clean, with no noise burst.
- **Notes** (in the result tabs) explain anything unusual in plain language.

## Optional: edge cases to try

- **Silent recording** (5 seconds of nothing) → clear "No narration found" error.
- **Music only, no voice** → the same clear error.
- **A .txt or .pdf file renamed to .mp3** → "audio is unreadable" error.
